Parent FAQs

When should we start growth monitoring?

From the first week after birth. Early tracking helps identify small issues before they become bigger concerns.

Do you support first-time parents?

Yes. Our coaching sessions are designed specifically for first-time parents who need practical daily support.

Can we get specialist referrals?

Yes. If needed, we coordinate referrals for pediatric specialists and follow-up communication.

Is virtual follow-up available?

Virtual follow-up is available for routine check-ins and progress guidance between physical visits.
