Platform Journey

How Novita Works From First Step to Ongoing Support

Novita is designed as a complete workflow, not a single-page service. Users can start with immediate needs, access experts, and continue long-term recovery with structured progress tracking.

Six-Step Support Lifecycle

A predictable process that keeps users, experts, and administrators aligned.

1

Create Account & Profile

Users register securely and set up a profile so the platform can personalize support pathways and content visibility.

2

Choose a Support Path

Users may enter recovery tracking, request expert services, access support resources, or combine all tracks based on current needs.

3

Submit Structured Requests

For professional services, users create structured requests so experts can review details, documents, and context efficiently.

4

Engage with Experts

Experts respond asynchronously, provide guidance, and maintain clear communication records across each request timeline.

5

Track Recovery Progress

In the recovery module, users complete daily check-ins, counseling sessions, and milestones while monitoring changes over time.

6

Review Outcomes & Adjust

Counselors and admins use analytics to identify risk patterns, adjust plans, and ensure better continuity and intervention quality.

How Features Connect Together

Each module is connected so users do not need to restart context at every step.

Profile Layer

Identity, role, and recovery profile establish baseline data for personalized support.

Care Layer

Plans, sessions, appointments, and relapse records keep treatment structured and auditable.

Service Layer

Expert requests and responses provide legal, cyber, medical, and investigative guidance.

Insight Layer

Dashboards and trends help stakeholders evaluate outcomes and intervene earlier.

Ready to Start?

Whether you need immediate expert help or a long-term recovery management flow, Novita lets you begin securely and continue with consistent support.
