About Novita

Novita is a social welfare platform helping people recover from addiction, access mental health support, and stay protected from digital harm. The name comes from nova, symbolizing new hope, new life, and new beginnings.

Our Core Services

  • Recovery & Mental Health: Progress tracking, mentorship, and therapy guidance.
  • Cyber Safety: Report abuse, harassment, blackmail, and get digital protection support.
  • Emergency Support: Fast links to crisis response, authorities, and counselors.

Our Mission

To bridge mental wellness and digital protection through one safe, private, and empowering digital platform for vulnerable individuals.

Our Vision

A world where no one feels alone in their struggles and everyone has access to hope, help, and healing.

Why Novita Matters
